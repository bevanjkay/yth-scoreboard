Scoreboard Updater

Instructions
Open the scoreboard
Click "Open / Refresh Scoreboard". It opens in a new window that you can drag to your second screen, TV or projector.
Update scores
Type a score or use the quick buttons. Changes save automatically and show on the scoreboard straight away.
Teams
Use "Add Team" and "Remove Team" for up to four teams. "Remove Team" always removes the last team.
Team colours
Pick a colour for each team. Names and scores are always white, so avoid very light colours.
Countdown
Enter a number of seconds and click "Start Countdown". Tick "Airhorn" to play a sound when it ends.
Resetting
Your teams and scores are remembered on this computer and browser. Click "Reset All" to start again.
